## Motion fixture provenance

The Motion fixture does not use paid APIs:

1. Two English utterances are rendered separately with local eSpeak.
2. Their WAV frame counts define exact synthetic sentence boundaries.
3. The utterances are concatenated into one real PCM WAV.
4. A synthetic dark raster is mapped to both scenes, proving background reuse without regenerating image bytes.
5. `mindframe motion` renders both Bilibili and Douyin preview outputs at scale 0.25.
6. FFprobe asserts codec, pixel format, frame rate, audio codec and dimensions; FFmpeg fully decodes both MP4s.

This is orchestration evidence, not a natural-voice, semantic, visual-quality, artistic-style or publishing-performance benchmark.
